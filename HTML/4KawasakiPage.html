Kawasaki motorcycles

Kawasaki, with its green machines, has left an indelible mark on the motorcycle world. Their Z650, now sleeker and lighter, boasts 68 horsepower.

Interestingly, Kawasaki started as a shipbuilding company. From ships to motorcycles, they’ve mastered the art of speed and power.
