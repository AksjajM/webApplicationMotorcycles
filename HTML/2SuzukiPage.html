Suzuki motorcycles

Suzuki, another Japanese giant, has been crafting motorcycles for decades. Their iconic SV650, with 76 horsepower, is a favorite among riders.

But Suzuki’s journey began with weaving looms and later expanded to cars and motorcycles. Today, they’re known for their sporty bikes and reliable engineering.
